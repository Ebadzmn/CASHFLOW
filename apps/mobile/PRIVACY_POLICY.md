# Privacy Policy for CashFlow Manager

**Effective Date:** August 26, 2026  
**Last Updated:** August 26, 2026  

Welcome to **CashFlow Manager** ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. If you have any questions or concerns about this privacy policy or our practices regarding your personal information, please contact us.

By using CashFlow Manager, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you voluntarily provide to us when you register on the app, express an interest in obtaining information about us or our products, or otherwise contact us.

### A. Personal Data Provided by You
* **Account Information:** Name, Email Address, and Password when creating an account.
* **Financial Data:** Income logs, expense entries, budgets, transaction notes, and categories you manually input.
* **Receipt Images & Uploads:** Photos or documents of receipts/bills you scan or upload using our optical recognition tools.

### B. Automatically Collected Data
* **Device & Usage Data:** Basic device information (OS version, device model) and app usage metrics to help us diagnose crashes and optimize performance.
* **Purchase Data:** Information regarding in-app purchases or subscriptions processed via Apple App Store / Google Play Store (we do not store your raw credit card or payment credentials).

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use personal information collected via our app for a variety of legitimate business purposes:
* **To Provide Core Services:** Managing your account, logging cashflow, processing receipt scans, and displaying budget analytics.
* **To Manage Subscriptions:** Verifying premium feature purchases via App Store in-app purchases.
* **To Improve Our App:** Analyzing usage trends and troubleshooting bugs to enhance overall user experience.
* **Security & Fraud Prevention:** Protecting user accounts from unauthorized access or malicious activity.
* **Customer Support:** Responding to support inquiries and user requests.

---

## 3. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We **do not sell, rent, or trade** your personal or financial data to third parties for marketing purposes.

We may share data only in the following limited situations:
* **Service Providers:** We may share data with trusted third-party cloud hosting, backend database providers, and OCR scanning engines strictly necessary to operate the app.
* **Legal Requirements:** We may disclose your information where required to do so by law or subpoena.
* **Business Transfers:** In connection with any merger, sale of company assets, or acquisition.

---

## 4. Data Security

We implement robust administrative, technical, and physical security measures to protect your personal and financial information. Sensitive data transmitted between your device and our servers is encrypted using standard Transport Layer Security (TLS/SSL).

However, please remember that no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

---

## 5. Data Retention & Deletion

We retain your personal data for as long as your account remains active or as needed to provide you with app services. 

### Account & Data Deletion
You have the right to request the deletion of your personal data and account at any time:
* You can delete your account directly through the **App Settings > Profile > Delete Account** section.
* Alternatively, you can email us to request complete data removal from our servers.
